```

Surrounding white chalk-like doodle icons: {ICONS}. Dotted arrows from text toward character.

Typography in Russian, handwritten casual Cyrillic:

Style: flat doodle, NOT 3D, NOT photorealistic, NOT anime. Grainy color fill on bean body, white line-art only. Square card layout, centered character, generous padding. Match VibeHunt personality-test infographic references.
```
